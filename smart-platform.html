💡 Design Quick Tips
🎨 60-30-10 Rule: Use your primary color for 60%, secondary for 30%, accent for 10% of your design.
✍️ Font Pairing: Use max 2-3 fonts. Pair a serif heading with a sans-serif body for classic elegance.
📐 White Space: Don't fear empty space — it makes designs breathe and feel premium.
👁️ Contrast: Text should have at least 4.5:1 contrast ratio with background for readability.
